# Output formats

Exact schemas. `/pmf log` writes or updates all three files; deviating breaks the linter and the
eval scorer.

Default location: the working directory. If the user names a project or vault folder, write
there instead. Never overwrite — read the existing file, merge, rewrite.

---

## `INSIGHTS.md`

```markdown
# Insights — <venture / persona>

_Last updated <date> · <N> calls · <N> insights · <N> refuted hypotheses_

## Sample

| Call | Who (anonymized) | Date | Length | Conflicts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Owner A — 6-bay independent shop | — | 20 min | none |
| C | Owner C — wants to join the project | — | excerpts | **advisor, not neutral** |

**Missing from this sample:** <who you have not talked to and must>

## Insights

### I-007 · Bay tablets are owner tools in technician costume
- **Evidence:** "we tried a tablet last winter and the techs stopped opening it after two weeks" — Owner A, [01:18]
- **Also:** "I made them use it. They never asked for it" — Owner A, [01:48]
- **Numbers:** ~2 weeks to zero use; abandoned in under a year
- **Changes:** every retention projection for this segment is fake until a tech asks to open it.
  Ship a technician-side reason, or sell it honestly as an owner tool.
- **Confidence:** 1 household, uncontradicted
- **Hypotheses:** H3 → REFUTED · H7 → OPEN
```

**Insight record rules**

- Stable ids (`I-007`). Never renumber; append.
- The **Evidence** line is a verbatim quote with speaker and timestamp. No quote, no insight.
- **Changes** is mandatory and must name a decision. If you cannot write it, delete the record —
  it is a summary.
- **Confidence** counts independent households or companies, not quotes. Two quotes from one
  person is still one household.
- A contradiction gets its own record cross-referencing the first (`contradicts I-007`), never a
  softened merge.

## Contamination section (same file, at the bottom)

```markdown
## Contaminated — not evidence

Call A: the interviewer described the product at **[18:00]**. Everything after is compliment-zone.

| Quote | Where | Why discarded |
|---|---|---|
| "Two hundred a month would be a magic number" | A [18:40] | invented price, post-pitch |
| "Okay, sign me up" | A [19:00] | post-pitch enthusiasm |
| "This was like a validation call" | B [03:00] | the interviewee's own verdict on the call |

**Net effect:** this corpus contains **no** willingness-to-pay evidence. Every price is stated,
none is a receipt.
```

Compliment-zone quotes appear **only** here. Quoting one anywhere else is a defect.

## `SCOREBOARD.md`

```markdown
# Hypothesis scoreboard — <venture>

_Updated <date> · <N> validated · <N> refuted · <N> open_

| id | Hypothesis | Status | Evidence | Next test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Independents will not touch anything that looks like dealer software | VALIDATED | I-003 | one dealer-group interview |
| H3 | Techs will use a tablet if the owner buys it | REFUTED | I-007 — dead in two weeks | — |
| H5 | Price is the blocker | REFUTED | I-011 — the blocker is another login | ask 3 owners what they cancelled and why |
| H8 | Growth runs through the jobber counter | OPEN | I-014 (1 shop) | ask 5 owners who they copy on tools |
```

Rules: exactly one status per row · one row per hypothesis · every row carries a **next test**
except refuted ones · new hypotheses generated by a call get appended as `OPEN` with the insight
that raised them.

## Next-interview questions (printed in chat, appended to `INSIGHTS.md`)

```markdown
## Next interview — sharpened

**What changed:** H3 and H5 are dead. Stop asking about price; start asking about the login
and catalog burden. We still have zero receipts, so every question below hunts for money already
spent.

**Who we need:** a dealer, a night-shift shop, a counter person. Ask each interviewee for these
by name.

1. What did you spend on parts software in the last year — go item by item?
2. Who restocked the last tablet that died, and what happened next?
3. Walk me through the last parts text you ignored.
…
```

Minimum 8 questions. At least two must aim at something this batch just taught you.

## Post-call scorecard (fill within 10 minutes, before the write-up)

- Who, household or company shape, and how you know them
- $/month in the category (number) · hours/day (number) · events/week (number)
- Purchase timeline: first purchase age or date + trigger; months until abandoned + what killed it
- Top pain in **their** words, verbatim
- Where their line is on the thing you were worried about
- Commitment secured: none / follow-up / intro / deposit
- Red-flag check: did I pitch, and when? did they compliment? was more than a third of the
  talking mine? did any answer contain zero specifics?
